# The Lamp

## Counting the steps

Mara climbed the two hundred and twelve steps the way her father had taught her: counting out loud, so the dark would know she was not afraid of it.

At the top, the lamp ticked like a patient clock. She saw the letters scratched into the lens: *Not here. Look below.*

* * *

By morning she had decided not to tell Tobias. Which was why, of course, he was the first person waiting at the bottom of the stairs.

## The quay

"Fog's a liar," Tobias said. "Makes near things far and far things near."
